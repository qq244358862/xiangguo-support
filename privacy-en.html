Privacy policy

About this policy

Xiangguo is operated by TIANMENG, an individual developer. Contact: hello@tianmengdesign.com. This policy applies to the current iPhone app and takes effect on October 8, 2026.

Your notes

The text, context, mood, date and voice notes you choose to save are stored in the app on your device, so you can read, play and search them. This version has no accounts, advertising or behavioral analytics services. Your notes are not uploaded to a developer-operated server, sold or used to train the developer’s models.

Voice input and voice notes

Voice input on the home screen uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition to turn speech into text. It does not save an audio recording or silently fall back to cloud recognition. “Record my voice” is a separate feature: audio is kept as an attachment only when you record and save a note. Permissions are requested when you use these features. You can keep typing if you decline and revoke microphone or speech recognition access in iPhone Settings. The microphone on Apple’s system keyboard is managed by Apple under its own terms.

Local search and components

Semantic search runs on your device using the bundled Jina embeddings v2 base zh model and ONNX Runtime. Its index stays in memory rather than in a separate persistent copy of your notes. The app also uses foundations such as Expo and React Native. The current app workflows do not send your notes to these components’ providers. Apple’s policies apply to its system services.

Privacy lock and file protection

Privacy lock is off by default and can be enabled in Settings. Apple verifies your identity; Xiangguo receives the result, not your face data or device passcode. You can use your device passcode if Face ID is unavailable. Saved notes and voice attachments use Apple Data Protection with Complete Protection. Security depends on iOS, your device settings and lock state. This does not mean closing the app wipes all memory or that every backup is end-to-end encrypted.

Backup, transfer and recovery

Notes, moods and saved voice attachments are eligible for system backup. If you enable iCloud Backup, Apple may store them in its cloud; computer backups are stored on your chosen computer. Transfer with Quick Start or restore a system backup that includes Xiangguo. Finish a backup first and check your notes and audio on the new phone before erasing the old one. The app cannot verify whether a system backup has completed. Installing the app on a new device without restoring or transferring data cannot recover your old notes. Enable encryption for computer backups. There is currently no separate account-based sync service.

Widgets and drafts

The widget opens a separate add-only flow that does not display existing notes. Entering the main app requires fresh verification when privacy lock is enabled. Moving a widget draft to the background closes it, discards unsaved text and attempts to delete temporary audio. Unreferenced audio in the saved-recordings directory is cleaned up the next time records are successfully loaded.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Saved content remains until you delete it or remove the app’s data. Deleting a card removes its record, associated audio that is not shared by another card, and its search index entry. Failed file cleanup is reported in the app with a retry option. Older system backups may retain earlier copies; the app cannot delete those backups, which you manage through Apple or your computer. You can view, copy text and delete records in the app. Contact the email above for questions about access, copies, corrections, deletion or permissions. The developer cannot directly access or recover local notes. Please avoid sending unnecessary private content in support emails.

Children and policy changes

The app is intended for people who can manage their own private notes and is not directed at children under 14. Guardians can contact the email above with privacy concerns. If future features such as cloud sync or analytics change how data is handled, this policy will be updated and notice and consent will be provided where applicable.
