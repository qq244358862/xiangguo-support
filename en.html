A few things you might wonder

Do I need to write every day?+

No. Keep a thought when you have something worth remembering. There are no daily check-ins or streaks to maintain.

Is one sentence really enough?+

One sentence can hold a whole lesson. If you want to keep more, add the backstory, save a voice note, or return later with another reflection.

Will it make decisions for me?+

Xiangguo helps you find your own experiences. An old lesson can be a useful reminder; whether it fits this moment is your call.

What happens when I change phones?+

Back up first, then transfer with iPhone Quick Start or restore a backup that includes Xiangguo. Check your notes and voice attachments on the new phone before erasing the old one. Reinstalling alone won’t restore your notes.
