How do I keep my notes when I change phones?

Use Apple Quick Start or restore a system backup that includes Xiangguo. Finish the backup and check notes and voice attachments on the new phone before erasing the old one. Reinstalling alone does not recover your notes.

What if Face ID or Touch ID is unavailable?

When privacy lock is enabled, you can use your iPhone’s device passcode through Apple’s authentication screen.

Does the developer receive my notes?

The current app stores your notes on your device and does not upload them to a developer-operated server. iCloud backups, if enabled, are managed by Apple. See the privacy policy for details.
